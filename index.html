1. Filler Words

  • Fillers (um, uh, like, you know) are ONLY allowed in Full Verbatim jobs.
  • In Clean / Edited Verbatim mode, all filler words must be stripped.

2. UI Buttons & Elements

  • Always capitalize and double quote interface buttons or menu items (e.g., click "Upload", press "Submit").

3. Numbers & Currency

  • Single digits zero to nine must be written as words; numbers 10 and above as digits.
  • Format currency correctly ($15, €27) and percentages as 5%.

4. Headers & Structure

  • Speaker tags must follow standard formatting (e.g., Male_1:, Female_1:).
  • Transcription job titles / topics must target exactly 7 words.
