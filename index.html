    📖AGLE General Guidelines Cheat Sheet
    
      
    
        
    1. Filler Words & Short Forms
    • Remove fillers and expand short forms ONLY in Clean Verbatim.
    • In Strict Verbatim, preserve exact spoken words, contractions, and stutters.
    
        
    2. UI Buttons
    • Capitalize and quote interface buttons (press "Upload").
    
        
    3. Numbers & Currency
    • 0 to 9 written as words; 10+ as digits.
    • Format currency as $15, €27. Percentage as 5%.
    
        
    4. Headers
    • Speaker tags match your input or custom mappings.
    • Topic headers target exactly 7 words.
