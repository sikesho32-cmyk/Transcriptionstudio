    Clean Read Overview
    
      
      
        
    • Ensure the transcript is concise and clear without interruptions.
      • 
        
    • Omit stutters, filler words, interruptions, partial words, and false starts.
      • 
        
    • Free of slang, irrelevant interjections, and non-verbal communication. Do not summarize or paraphrase.
      • 
        
    • Maintain sentence meaning. Example: I felt like kind of a fraud.
      • 
        
    • "So" and "And" rules: If the speaker begins many thoughts with So or And, omit them unless they form a logical link. Never place a comma directly after starting "So". Example: So it should be clear to you how humans evolved from apes.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Verbatim Overview
    
      
      
        
    • Includes all dialogue word by word, including fillers, partial words, false starts, slang, stutters, and repetitions.
      • 
        
    • Use dashes for stutters, partial words, and false starts. Stutter: I- I- I'm going shopping. Partial Word: Close the window, I'm fr- freezing.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Filler Words
    
      
      
        
    • Fillers are only added in Verbatim jobs (e.g., you know, um, uh-huh, like, all right, sort of, kind of).
      • 
        
    • Always place commas around filler words in Verbatim. Example: They went, um, to the store.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Platform Rules
    
      
      
        
    • Work only in the platform; do not use external editors like Microsoft Word.
      • 
        
    • If speaker names are not listed, use MALE_1, FEMALE_1, or UNKNOWN_1.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Audio Rules
    
      
      
        
    • If 80% or more is in a foreign language, unassign due to "Unsupported Language".
      • 
        
    • Transcribe every lyric being sung verbatim if music is part of the speaker's performance. Do not transcribe background music.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Punctuation
    
      
      
        
    • Use double quotation marks for quotes. Use Oxford comma in lists of three or more.
      • 
        
    • Exclamation marks are never used (except established company names like Yahoo!). No ellipsis (...).
      • 
        
    • Keep contractions exactly as spoken. Do not add accent marks or special letters not found on a standard keyboard.
      •

    Writing Rules
    
      
      
        
    • Use original speaker words. Default spelling is US spelling.
      • 
        
    • Required standard forms: email (not e-mail), all right (not alright), okay (not O.K), yeah (not yep, yup), y'all (not ya'll), I'm (not Am), TV / US / UK (not T.V. / U.S. / U.K.).
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Numbers
    
      
      
        
    • 0–9 written as words (zero, six, seven); 10 and up as digits (10, 47, 126).
      • 
        
    • Decimals: 3.5 (not three point five). Currency: $15, €27, 33 cents.
      • 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Mathematical Guidelines
    
      
      
        
    • Use digits and symbols for math equations. Within regular speech, use words for symbols.
      • 
        
    • Symbols: + (plus), - (minus/negative), * (times), / (divided by), = (equals), () (parentheses).
