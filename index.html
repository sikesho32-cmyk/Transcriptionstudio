    📖Anti-Rejection Guidelines Cheat Sheet
    
      
    
        
    1. Run-on Sentences
    • Keep sentences concise. If a sentence exceeds 30 words without a pause or conjunction break, split it cleanly at natural punctuation or clauses.
    
        
    2. Compound Modifiers
    • Hyphenate compound adjectives preceding a noun (e.g., high-quality micro samples, two-year contract, price-sensitive market).
    
        
    3. Punctuation & Quotes
    • Ensure every sentence ends with proper terminal punctuation (. ! ?). Keep quotation marks clean and correctly formatted.
    
        
    4. Continuous Timestamps
    • Every segment end time equals the exact start time of the next segment, running continuously to the end of the audio file.
